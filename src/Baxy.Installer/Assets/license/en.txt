5. Privacy

By default, the language model, the speech recognition and the voice run on your computer, and the developer receives nothing of yours: BAXY has no server of its own and sends no usage statistics. When you ask BAXY for something that needs an internet service, only what that request needs leaves your computer: what you ask to search for, to DuckDuckGo, Wikipedia or Photon (OpenStreetMap data), with your city and its country if you ask for something "near me"; the place's coordinates and your IP address, to MET Norway, for the weather; your IP, to ipwho.is, to find your city; the currencies, to Frankfurter; the video you ask for, to YouTube; the title of a game you ask to install or to see in the Steam store and that is not among your installed games, to that store (Valve); the page you ask to read or the file you ask to download, to its site; your IP to Hugging Face if you choose to download the optional fine-tuned model; and, if you search for or install a model in Settings → Model → Local, the name you type and your IP to Hugging Face, and your IP to GitHub to download Ollama. What you ask to open, send, install or run with your browser or another program of yours (for example a site, a message through Discord or WhatsApp, an email through Outlook, a program with winget or a terminal command) leaves through that program, under its terms; and if you ask to check whether a machine answers, it receives a ping from your IP. Without being asked, BAXY sends its version and your IP to GitHub (api.github.com) to look for a new version, once at each start, unless you switch off «Tell me about new versions» in Settings → General → Version; the same is sent when you check for updates or ask BAXY, and if you choose to update, the new installer is downloaded from GitHub and checked against its SHA-256. The installer downloads BAXY's parts from GitHub and, if Windows lacks them, WebView2 and the Visual C++ runtime from Microsoft; if WebView2 is missing at start, BAXY downloads it from Microsoft without asking. licenses/PRIVACIDAD.md says what each service receives and links its terms. If you choose to connect your own key from an AI company in Settings, or another server under «Other» that is not on your computer, then while that option is on, what you say and what BAXY reads for you (including text and images from your screen, files and web pages) is sent to that company or server, under its terms and at your own cost. What BAXY remembers is stored on your computer, and you can view, edit and delete it. BAXY also leaves unencrypted technical logs in its data folder (%LOCALAPPDATA%\BAXY). The two that note each turn (renew\turns.jsonl, up to about 100 MB, and 1\conversation\conversation.v1.jsonl, up to 8 MB) keep the time, how long it took and which operations were used, not what you say nor what BAXY answers, unless you turn that on in your computer to diagnose a fault (the BAXY_TRACE_WORDS=1 variable); an error log may quote a fragment of what the model was writing when it failed. What an earlier version of BAXY had already noted in them, with what you said, stays there until you use «Forget everything». When BAXY looks at your screen it keeps the capture, unencrypted, in 1\captures only while it handles that request: it deletes it when the request ends and when BAXY closes. «Forget everything» deletes those logs and the captures along with the conversations.
