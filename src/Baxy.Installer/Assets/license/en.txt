4. BAXY acts on your computer

BAXY opens programs, clicks, types, reads windows and changes settings on your computer when you ask it to. In its normal mode it asks for confirmation before destructive actions (deleting, overwriting, closing unsaved work, buying, sending messages to other people). A bypass mode, which you turn on yourself, removes those confirmations. You are responsible for what you ask BAXY to do and for turning bypass mode on.

5. Privacy

By default, the language model, the speech recognition and the voice run on your computer, and BAXY does not send what you say or your files to any service, though it may search the web to answer you. If you choose to connect your own key from an AI company in Settings, then while that option is on, what you say and what BAXY reads for you (including text and images from your screen, files and web pages) is sent to that company, under that company's terms and at your own cost. What BAXY remembers is stored on your computer, and you can view, edit and delete it.
