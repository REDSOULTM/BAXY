Microsoft Visual C++ runtime in BAXY's package

Files: msvcp140.dll, msvcp140_1.dll, msvcp140_2.dll, msvcp140_atomic_wait.dll, vcruntime140.dll and vcruntime140_1.dll,
in python\, llama\ and llama-vulkan\. They are not BAXY's and not under its MIT license: Microsoft distributes them as
"Distributable Code" of Visual Studio. scripts/build_steam.ps1 copies them, unmodified and with Microsoft's signature checked,
from the Visual Studio of the machine that builds the package (-VisualCppRedist). BAXY 1.3.1 took them from
Visual Studio 2022 Community, VC\Redist\MSVC\14.42.34433\x64\Microsoft.VC143.CRT.
