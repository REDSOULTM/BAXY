repository at revision `a1fe7427`, with the metadata sherpa-onnx needs from tpmaerk's at revision `b86784a3`; the weights
were not meant to change. It is 470 bytes larger than Olicorne's file at that revision, and its hash matches neither.

Supertonic 3, Copyright (c) 2026 Supertone Inc.: <https://huggingface.co/Supertone/supertonic-3>. Licensed under the BigScience Open
RAIL-M License dated August 18, 2022 (`OpenRAIL-M-Supertonic-3.txt`). **The use restrictions in its Attachment A apply

Spanish one, where the English wording prevails. The full license is in `OpenRAIL-M-Supertonic-3.txt`.

The int8 conversion for sherpa-onnx comes from
<https://huggingface.co/csukuangfj2/sherpa-onnx-supertonic-3-tts-int8-2026-05-11>: `duration_predictor.int8.onnx`,
`text_encoder.int8.onnx`, `vector_estimator.int8.onnx`, `vocoder.int8.onnx` and `unicode_indexer.bin` have the same
SHA-256 as that repository, and `tts.json` the same git blob, all unchanged.

`voice.bin`: **changed by the BAXY project.** It is a blend of three of the model's voice styles (`baxy-voice.json`
records the weights). `baxy-voice.json` was added by the BAXY project.

**BAXY's voice is synthetic: it is generated by a machine and is not a person's voice.**

## Language model (`models/`): Apache-2.0

Qwen3.5-4B, by the Qwen team: <https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.5-4B> (`Qwen3.5-4B-LICENSE.txt`). The repository has
no `NOTICE` file.

`BAXY-Qwen3.5-4B-ft-c3-c4c2_1-sA-Q4_K_M.gguf`: **changed by the BAXY project.** It is Qwen3.5-4B fine-tuned with a LoRA,
merged and quantized to Q4_K_M.

`Qwen3.5-4B-mmproj-F16.gguf`: same SHA-256 as `mmproj-F16.gguf` in <https://huggingface.co/unsloth/Qwen3.5-4B-GGUF>,
unchanged except for its file name.

## Wake word (`wake/`): attribution given as a precaution

`head.onnx` is the BAXY project's own network. According to its training notes it carries no weights from Kroko (compared on
2026-11-06 with `encoder_kroko_es.onnx`: none of its 127 tensors of 64 values or more equals one of Kroko's 944), but it
was trained imitating the Kroko ASR encoder, by Banafo: <https://huggingface.co/Banafo/Kroko-ASR>. Kroko describes its community models as «CC-BY-SA»
without giving a version, and the `LICENSE` file in that repository is empty. Whether a network trained this way counts
as an adaptation has not been settled, so this attribution is given as a precaution and `CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt` is included
for reference, on the assumption that the current version applies.
